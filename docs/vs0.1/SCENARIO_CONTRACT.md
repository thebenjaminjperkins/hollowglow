# VS0.1 — Vertical-slice scenario contract

2026-11-17. Planning deliverable; no new playable profile is registered.
Intended-design authority: [revised Vision](../HOLLOWLIGHT_VISION_UPDATE_DRAFT.md).
The owner's VS0.1 approval resolves C1/C3 as specification only and C2 as minimum
spatial contracts before combat; substantial generation variety follows the first
connected combat/recovery demonstration. C4 remains unresolved.

## Experience and environment

The eventual demonstration is a 15–20-minute cooperative test scenario, not the
duration or balance of the complete game. Use the existing opt-in sanctuary hall,
mountain threshold, road approach and Cinder garage. Preserve the compact/alternate,
M6a, garage and hall fixtures. No new themed location, full procedural overhaul or
default-layout replacement is required for the first Bloom encounter.

Two Linux desktop instances connect directly as host and client. Keyboard/mouse
and controller remain design requirements; independent input, camera and menus
belong to each local actor. No permanent caretaker. Roles below are examples and
may switch; both players should have useful choices at home and outside.

| Beat | Player activity and consequential choice | Future work owner / evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Establish home | Inspect shared supplies, withdraw seeds, plant four starting plots, manually load/start generator and allocate beacon/workbench/lights | Reuse existing rules; VS2 connects actors, VS1 accepts whole-scene presentation |
| Prepare expedition | Choose what to carry in six slots, reserve return capacity, agree tool/weapon roles | VS2 actor ownership; VS5 equipment/control decision; no free gear granted by this document |
| Investigate garage | Follow readable road, discover architecture, search finite contents, use transferable tool, weigh machinery/van dismantling against preservation | Existing M6b loop; VS5 adds one readable Bloom threat and disturbance response |
| Return and invest | Deposit/withdraw explicitly, choose one useful infrastructure project, deliver locally, assemble briefly and commission; harvest/replant when mature | VS4 construction; exact project/material access blocked below |
| Prepare and evacuate | Read beacon progress, maintain power, choose route and loadout, travel under threat, board rescue transport | VS7 nonterminal signal readiness and departure outcome |

Quiet travel, conversation and planning remain valid. Do not enforce activity by
lengthening roads, holds, searches or assembly timers. Combat must be satisfying
and legible, not a material tax. Home must look inhabited and feel relieving;
ordinary Bloom cannot bypass intact walls and closed manual bunker doors.

## Scope and unsatisfied prerequisites

The eventual slice includes farming, power management, one meaningful **infrastructure**
investment, purposeful salvage, one deliberate weapon, one readable Bloom enemy,
recoverable equipment, limited emergency resurrection and dangerous extraction.
Four initial plots, existing recipes/yields/consumption/resources stay unchanged
until an explicit subsequent approval. Basic crops gain no grow-light requirement.
No full crafting catalog, vehicle restoration, bosses, PvP or routine durability.

**Recommended investment candidate:** one additional growing plot, reusing the
existing bed kit and fifth-plot function as the regression baseline. This is not
approval to add raw-material assembly, relocate its socket or change its recipe.
Physical-delivery/blueprint commissioning needs a reviewed VS4 mapping that charges
materials exactly once. Do not charge both a complete kit and its ingredients.

**Material blocker:** the garage has Scrap, Food, Fuel and a prototype tool, but no
Organic. Preserving the van recovers ten Scrap, below the bed's twelve-Scrap cost.
At the proposed accelerated timing, two Glowcaps provide only two Organic before
minute 20, below the bed's four and backpack's three. A backpack is also not a
substitute for infrastructure merely because it is craftable. See the
[worksheet](TIMING_WORKSHEET.md). No investment is credited as completed.

Options requiring owner approval before a runnable scenario:

1. **Recommended:** permit a small detour to the existing Organic source at the
   hollow and, if preserving the van, the existing hut Scrap source. Reuse finite
   allocations; do not invent garage Organic or new scenery. Measure the hall-route
   detours before committing to the short-session envelope.
2. Keep strictly to hall/road/garage and revise the demonstration scope/timing in
   a separately reviewed proposal. Current recipes cannot deliver the investment
   by minute 20. Do not secretly change recipes, pre-grow crops or grant materials.

The recommendation broadens visits to existing locations, so it is **not selected**
by this contract. Weapon acquisition and extraction destination/window are likewise
unapproved. The contract defines their purpose, not free inventory or new world nodes.

## Success run and dedicated recovery test

An ordinary successful playthrough must not require someone to die. It should show
a real home–expedition–return cycle, harvest/replant, manual allocation, a meaningful
investment, a Bloom encounter with retreat/engagement choice and final evacuation.
No requirement to kill every enemy, dismantle the van or explore every location.

Separate dedicated test, once VS5/VS6 exist:

1. Two living actors begin with recorded gear; one dies at a reachable known point.
2. Exactly one persistent death backpack retains the carried contents. The survivor
   can find it and retrieve some/all items subject to real carrying limits.
3. A limited emergency recovery returns the dead actor without duplicating gear;
   remaining charges, activation and waiting behavior use explicitly approved rules.
4. Test simultaneous/last-survivor deaths separately: true full-party death ends
   immediately even if resurrection resources remain. Do not infer all-downed or
   disconnected-player eligibility. Those cases remain blocked pending C4 decisions.

The damaged resurrection chamber stays inert until its own approved work. Full
repair-to-renewable Biomass resurrection is not required for this initial test.
Optional botanical oddities do not become assumed mechanics.

## Outcomes and unresolved boundaries

Transmission establishes an opportunity, not victory; time/power/crops keep running.
First departure with one or more aboard ends the run: full rescue if everyone
escapes, partial rescue otherwise. An empty departure with survivors remains
nonterminal and needs costly re-communication plus a feasible recovery objective.
VS7 must define that small branch; it may exceed the ordinary 15–20-minute target.
No minute-20 automatic defeat or arbitrary fuel-loss game-over.

Unresolved until their dependent milestones: extraction window/destination/disclosure,
second-chance costs and subsequent misses, death/boarding event arbitration,
all-downed behavior and disconnected-player eligibility. A session ending on host
loss is a separate technical proposal, not an automatic in-fiction party wipe.

## Connected-play assumptions for later approval

Confirmed: two Linux desktop instances, direct host/client, host validates shared
simulation; keyboard/mouse and controller supported as design requirements.

Proposed narrow VS2 assumptions, **not approved policies**: both players join before
the run, no join-in-progress or reconnect, no host migration, client receives a clear
session-ended message on host loss. Client disconnect should stop the proof with
an explicit diagnostic rather than silently applying a survival/death outcome.
Approve runtime disconnect behavior before enabling combat. No save/resume, public
lobby, NAT traversal service or dedicated deployment is promised by direct connect.

## Human acceptance

Whole-scene visual checkpoint and first two-human connected-play checkpoint are
mandatory. Technical checks alone cannot accept square masking, floating props,
unconvincing joins, unreadable combat or dull activity. Observe curiosity, worthwhile
choices, teamwork, idle/chore time, menu friction and relief on returning home.
Ask whether the pair wants another run. Record actual time instead of forcing the
target. Recovery is evaluated by the dedicated test, not a compulsory scripted death.
